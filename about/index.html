About TerraVellia

TerraVellia is a small garden idea blog with one job: help you grow a garden you love. Every article is a visual list of ideas — raised beds, containers, herbs, flowers, indoor plants and seasonal planting — written in plain language for real homes, real soil and real weather.

We started TerraVellia because most garden advice online is either too vague or too technical. We wanted something in between: ideas you can picture, save to Pinterest, and actually build on a weekend. Each idea comes with practical detail — bed sizes, soil mixes, sunlight, spacing, watering and the plants that suit them — so you know exactly what to do next.

We are not professional landscapers. We are home gardeners who test ideas in our own beds and pots, keep what works, and share it here. If an idea fails in our garden, it does not make it onto the site.

Questions or suggestions? Get in touch — we read every message.
