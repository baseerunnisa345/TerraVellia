Key Takeaways

  • One portrait image per idea, in a 2:3 box, with a unique alt text.
  • Numbered sections with a table of contents that jumps to each idea.
  • Takeaways and FAQs close every article, with matching JSON-LD.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many ideas does a TerraVellia article have?

Real articles have between 25 and 40 numbered ideas. The admin dashboard blocks export outside that range, and the title count must match the number of sections exactly.

Where do the article images come from?

Images are processed in the admin dashboard: cropped to 2:3, resized to 1000×1500 and compressed to WebP under 100KB before they are added to the repository.
