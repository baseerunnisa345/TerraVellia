Indoor Plant Ideas

Indoor plants bring life to every room, clean the air and ask for very little in return. These ideas cover low-light survivors, statement floor plants, trailing vines for shelves and easy-care collections for beginners. You will learn which plants suit each room, how much light, water and humidity they need, when to repot and propagate, and how to spot trouble early — yellow leaves, root rot and pests. Whether you want one hardy plant or a full indoor jungle, there is a green companion here for your home.
