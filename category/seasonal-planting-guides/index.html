Seasonal Planting Guides

Good gardens follow the seasons. These guides tell you what to plant, sow and harvest in spring, summer, autumn and winter, so you always know what to do next. You will find month-by-month checklists, last frost dates explained simply, hardiness zones and sowing calendars, and succession sowing ideas for keeping colour and food coming all year. Follow the season, work with your climate, and your garden will reward you in every month of the year.
