Herb Garden Ideas

Fresh herbs turn ordinary cooking into something special, and they are among the easiest plants to grow. These ideas cover windowsill pots in full sunlight, kitchen-door herb spirals, shady mint patches and full herb beds, with tips on soil, watering, pruning and harvesting so your plants stay productive all season. Grow basil, rosemary, mint, parsley and more — learn which herbs are perennial, which need re-sowing each spring, and how to dry or freeze the harvest for winter cooking.
