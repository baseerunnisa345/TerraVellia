Container Gardening Ideas

No yard? No problem. Container gardening lets you grow herbs, vegetables and flowers on a balcony, patio, doorstep or sunny windowsill. These ideas show which pots, potting mix, compost and drainage work best — never garden soil in pots — how often to water, and how to group plants that like the same sunlight and soil. From self-watering planters to stacked strawberry towers, you will find practical, low-cost ways to start growing today, even if all you have is one sunny corner.
